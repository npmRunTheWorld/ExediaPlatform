    Proposal for Exedia Platforms

    Make Exedia the place to find human-made work

    Today Exedia is a Pinterest-style board app. Pinterest already does that at a scale of hundreds of millions of users, so copying it won't win. What Pinterest users complain about most is AI-generated filler and work that loses its maker's name once it gets reposted. Exedia can be built around fixing exactly that.

    Why this direction

    Through 2025 and into 2026, Pinterest users reported feeds full of AI images that link to spam sites. Pinterest responded with an "AI modified" label and a See Fewer control, but creators say hand-drawn work still gets tagged by mistake. Smaller tools like Are.na, Savee and Cosmos have grown by being calmer and curated by people. None of them make proof of process and credit the center of the product.

    Exedia today

    • Generic save-an-image boards
    • Endless feed with no ordering logic
    • Credit is a small avatar that disappears on repost
    • Admin check runs in the browser

    Exedia after the revamp

    • Every piece shows who made it and how
    • Following feed that ends, plus a daily edit picked by people
    • Credit chain follows the work through every save
    • All writes go through a server, never the browser

    Edge cases to plan for

    • People will lie on the AI form. Pair the declaration with reports from viewers and a review queue, and treat process shots as the stronger signal.
    • Most phone photos and screenshots carry no Content Credentials. A missing credential must never read as a sign of guilt.
    • Process shots can be faked as well. The stamp means the maker showed their steps. It doesn't prove anything beyond that, and the copy should say so.
    • Reposts of work from other sites need a source link, and a takedown path for makers who want their work removed.
